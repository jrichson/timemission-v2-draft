Got Questions?

Frequently Asked

How far in advance should we book peak-season dates?

For December 1 through December 23, we recommend booking six to eight weeks out. Weekday slots are typically more available than weekends.

How big a group can you host?

From 10-person small teams up to 300+ full-venue buyouts, depending on the location. Let us know your count and we'll confirm what's possible.

Is catering available?

Varies by location. Many venues have holiday-ready food and drink packages; others work with outside caterers. We'll share what's available at your selected location.

Can we organize a gift exchange or festive extras?

Most locations can accommodate gift exchange space and festive add-ons, depending on venue setup. Let us know what you're planning.

Is a full-venue buyout available?

Yes, most locations offer buyouts for larger holiday parties. Pricing and availability depend on the location and date.

What ages can play?

Ages 6 and up. Kids 6–13 need an adult in the group. Perfect for mixed-age family holiday parties.

Ready to upgrade the holiday party?

Peak-season dates book fast, send us your date and headcount and we'll lock it in.
