Before You Visit

What should I know before visiting Time Mission?

Book through the venue location page, wear comfortable clothing and closed-toe shoes, review the age/team rules, and use group-event pages for parties or private events.

Read the full pre-visit guidance

Before visiting Time Mission, guests should book through the location page for the venue they plan to attend and review that page for current hours, ticket options, and group packages. Mission sessions are built for teams, repeat attempts, and a mix of physical, skill, logic, and coordination challenges. Age rules are important: younger players may need an adult present, while older children and teens can often participate more independently depending on the venue policy. Comfortable clothing and closed-toe shoes are recommended because several missions involve movement, balance, or fast reactions. Larger groups can usually split into teams, rotate through missions, and compare scores. For birthdays, corporate events, school trips, or private events, use the group-event pages or contact form so Time Mission can match the visit format to the group size and occasion beforehand.
