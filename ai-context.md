### What is Time Mission?

Time Mission is a live, team-based entertainment experience where groups of two to five players move through 25 or more short mission rooms during a timed session. Each room is a self-contained challenge lasting about one to five minutes, so players can choose the order, replay favorites, and build a score across the venue. The format blends escape-room puzzles, arcade-style scoring, physical tasks, and cooperative problem solving without locking a group into one long scenario. Sessions are sold in 60, 90, or 120 minute lengths, with most groups completing 15 to 20 missions in a 90 minute visit. Time Mission works for families, friends, birthdays, team outings, field trips, date nights, and private events because different rooms test speed, strength, skill, memory, logic, and coordination. The experience is designed so mixed-ability groups can contribute in different ways rather than relying on one kind of player.

## What Is Time Mission?

Time Mission combines escape-room style puzzles, arcade-style scoring, and physical challenge rooms into one timed team session. Unlike a traditional escape room, teams do not stay in one locked scenario; they choose from many portals, replay favorites, and compete for a score across the venue.

## Key Facts

- Session lengths: Sessions come in 60, 90, or 120 minutes. More time = more portals to conquer. Pick your adventure.
- Ages: Time Mission is perfect for ages 6 to 106! Kids 6–13 need an adult in the group. Ages 14+ can play on their own.
- Team size: Teams are between 2 to 5 players. Bigger crew? If you come with more than five people, we will simply break you into multiple teams and you play against each other for bragging rights.
- Mission format: Missions are individual challenge rooms, each with a unique puzzle or mission to solve. Every mission takes 1–5 minutes, and you can play them in any order or replay your favorites.
- Mission count: 25–30 depending on your Time Mission location. Most groups tackle 15–20 missions in a 90-minute session.
- Physical intensity: It's a mix. Some missions are brain teasers, others get you moving (throwing balls, dodging lasers, pressing buttons). Your team picks which portals to play, so you control the intensity. Meters outside each room show what skills you'll need before you walk in.
- Dress code: Closed-toe shoes required (no sandals). Sneakers and flexible clothing are your best bet since about half the portals get physical.
- Arrival timing: Your session starts at your booked time, so arriving 10–15 minutes early is recommended. Groups of 30 or more should arrive 30 minutes early. Late arrivals may be rebooked or have a shorter session.
- Waiver requirement: Yes, everyone entering the portal area needs a signed waiver on file. For players under 18, a parent or legal guardian must sign.
- Military discount: Yes, 10% off with a valid military ID. Since we need to verify the ID in person, this discount is walk-in only, not available on advance reservations.
