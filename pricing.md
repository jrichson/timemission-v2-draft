# Time Mission Pricing And Booking Facts

## Direct Answer

Time Mission pricing should be treated as location-specific and checkout-specific rather than a single universal ticket price. Standard admission depends on the selected venue, date, session length, party size, and available packages, so the live booking flow for each open location is the authoritative source for exact prices. Sessions are available in 60, 90, or 120 minute lengths, and advance booking is recommended because online checkout guarantees the selected time when inventory is available. Larger groups can book group tickets online, while custom events, party rooms, buyouts, catering, extended hours, and branded experiences require a quote. Military guests can receive 10 percent off with a valid military ID, but that discount is walk-in only because the ID must be verified in person. Gift cards never expire and can be used for admission, parties, corporate events, and merchandise.

## Standard Admission Facts

- Session lengths: Sessions come in 60, 90, or 120 minutes. More time = more portals to conquer. Pick your adventure.
- Team size: Teams are between 2 to 5 players. Bigger crew? If you come with more than five people, we will simply break you into multiple teams and you play against each other for bragging rights.
- Advance booking: We recommend it, especially on weekends. Walk-ins are welcome based on availability, but booking online guarantees your spot.
- Arrival timing: Your session starts at your booked time, so arriving 10–15 minutes early is recommended. Groups of 30 or more should arrive 30 minutes early. Late arrivals may be rebooked or have a shorter session.
- Military discount: Yes, 10% off with a valid military ID. Since we need to verify the ID in person, this discount is walk-in only, not available on advance reservations.

## Group And Event Pricing

- Group booking: The easiest way is to book group tickets directly through our online system. Just select your location, pick a date, and add your group. For larger or custom events, submit an event request and our team will be in touch.
- Minimum group size: The minimum is two players. For private events and venue rentals, most locations can have up to 125 simultaneous players. Submit an event request to learn more.
- Corporate capacity: From 8-person team offsites up to 125 full-venue buyouts, depending on the location. Send us your headcount and we'll confirm what's possible at your chosen venue.
- Buyout pricing: Custom pricing based on venue, date, duration, and headcount. Request a quote and we'll send a detailed proposal.
- Buyout minimum: Varies by location, but most locations do not require a minimum headcount. Send us your size and we'll confirm what works at your chosen venue.

## Gift Card Facts

- Expiration: No! Time Mission gift cards never expire. The recipient can use them whenever they're ready for their adventure.
- Use cases: Gift cards can be used for general admission tickets, birthday party packages, corporate events, and any merchandise at our locations.
- Refund policy: Gift cards are non-refundable but they never expire, so the recipient can use them whenever they're ready!
