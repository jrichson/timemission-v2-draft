OUR STORY

THE GAME IS REAL

Part escape room, part video game, all real. Teams of 2-5 compete through 25+ immersive missions. Grab your crew, race the clock, and play as many as you can.

OUR GAME

WHAT'S THE MISSION?

Time Mission is an immersive social gaming venue where teams of 2-5 compete through 25+ missions. Every mission is a different game, from brain-bending puzzles to full-body physical challenges. No headsets, no controllers, no sitting around. You are the game.

Choose your session length, pick your missions, and play as many as you can. Each one lasts just a few minutes, so you keep moving, keep competing, and keep the energy high.

Birthdays, team outings, field trips, bachelorette blowouts, or a crew out to settle who's really the best. Time Mission is built for every team, every occasion.

GET STARTED

HOW IT WORKS

01

BOOK YOUR SESSION

Pick your group size, choose how long you want to play, and reserve your spot online.

02

CHECK IN

Arrive at your location, check in at the front desk, and get your wristbands. You're almost on the clock.

03

MISSION BRIEFING

Watch the briefing, learn how the challenges work, and get ready to hit the floor.

04

PLAY

Pick your missions, race the clock, and play as many rooms as you can. Every door is a new challenge.

PLAN YOUR MISSION

BEFORE YOU ARRIVE

BOOK IN ADVANCE

We recommend booking ahead to guarantee your spot. Walk-ins are welcome but subject to availability.

DRESS TO PLAY

Wear comfortable clothes you can run, jump, and crawl in. Closed-toe shoes with flat soles required.

ARRIVE EARLY

Show up 10-15 minutes before your session to check in and get briefed. Don't miss a single second.

BRING YOUR CREW

Teams of 2-5 players per challenge. The more people, the more fun, and the more rooms you can conquer.
